# organise (moving and renaming documents) worklog

## 2026-11-12 choosing and organising documents in Recents
Ctrl or Command-click and Shift-click choose documents in Recents (a plain
click still opens one), and a bar offers Move to Folder…, Copy to…,
Rename, Show in File Explorer, Remove from Recents and Move to Recycle Bin.
The folder picker lists pinned folders, search folders and recently used
places (kept in local storage, a per-window convenience) and filters as you
type. A name clash asks with the Finder's words and offers Keep Both,
Replace and Skip; Replace is never chosen for the person. The toast's Undo
calls the shell, which really moves the files back. F2, Ctrl Shift M and
Delete start rename, move and recycle; Delete always asks first.
